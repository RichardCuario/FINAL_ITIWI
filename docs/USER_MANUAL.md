# iTIWI User Manual

**System:** iTIWI Mobile Application  
**Audience:** Residents, visitors, and other mobile-app users  
**Document status:** Based on the current application implementation  
**Date:** 21 August 2026

## 1. About iTIWI

iTIWI is a mobile application for accessing local information and selected municipal services. It provides:

- Barangay information and local updates
- News and announcements
- Tourist destinations and local places
- Emergency hotline numbers
- Transparency documents
- Frequently asked questions and legal notices
- Concern and incident reporting
- Online certificate appointment requests
- Notifications and personal account management

Public information can be viewed from the app. Reports, My Reports, and Online Services require a successful identity verification.

## 2. Requirements

Before using the app, make sure that:

1. The app is installed on a supported mobile device.
2. You have an internet connection for sign-in, submissions, current content, and status updates.
3. You have an email address for account access and password recovery.
4. You can provide a valid ID when identity verification is required.

Keep the app updated and allow notifications if you want to receive status updates promptly.

## 3. First Launch and Onboarding

When the app is opened for the first time, onboarding screens introduce the application. Use these controls:

- **Get started:** Continue to account access.
- **Next:** Move to the next onboarding screen.
- **Skip:** Go directly to account access.
- **Done:** Finish onboarding.

After onboarding, the app opens the sign-in or registration screen.

## 4. Create an Account

1. Open the registration or sign-up option.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter a username.
4. Enter a password of at least six characters.
5. Read the **Privacy Policy**, **Terms of Use**, and **Data & Compliance** notice.
6. Select the legal-consent checkbox.
7. Submit the form.
8. Open the email sent by iTIWI/Firebase and verify your email address.
9. Return to the app and sign in.

Registration cannot be completed without the required account fields and legal consent. Use a valid email address that you can access.

## 5. Sign In

### Email and password

1. Enter your email address.
2. Enter your password.
3. Select **Sign In**.

### Google sign-in

1. Select Google sign-in.
2. Choose your Google account.
3. Accept the legal notices when prompted if you have not already done so.

Google sign-in depends on the device and Firebase configuration. Facebook sign-in is not currently active in the mobile app.

### Reset a forgotten password

1. Enter your email address on the sign-in screen.
2. Select **Forgot password?**.
3. Open the password-reset email.
4. Follow the link and create a new password.
5. Return to the app and sign in with the new password.

If no email arrives, check spam or junk mail and confirm that the email address is correct.

## 6. Home Screen and Navigation

The main navigation contains **Home**, **News**, and **Profile**. The Home screen also provides shortcuts and search.

### Home shortcuts

- **Barangay:** Browse barangay information, officials, descriptions, and local details.
- **Tourist Guide:** Explore destinations and local places.
- **Emergency:** View emergency hotlines, including police, fire, rescue, and other listed contacts.
- **Transparency:** Open public documents and transparency records.
- **Report:** Submit a concern or incident.
- **Online Service:** Open available digital services.

### Drawer menu

The drawer provides access to Home, News, Barangay, Tourist Guide, Transparency, Report Issue, Online Services, Emergency Hotline, Profile, Help & FAQ, and Logout.

### Search

Use the search field to find features, barangays, news, FAQs, reports, profile pages, certificate services, and other available destinations. Select a result to open it.

## 7. Browse Public Information

### News

1. Open **News** from the bottom navigation, drawer, or search.
2. Browse published announcements and updates.
3. Select a news item to read its full details.

### Barangay information

1. Open **Barangay**.
2. Select a barangay from the list or search results.
3. Review the available description, officials, location, and contact information.

### Tourist Guide

1. Open **Tourist Guide**.
2. Browse or search destinations.
3. Select a destination to view its description, location, contact details, website, and available images.

### Emergency Hotlines

1. Open **Emergency Hotline**.
2. Select the relevant agency or category.
3. Use the displayed phone number to place a call. Confirm the number and your location before calling when circumstances allow.

### Transparency

1. Open **Transparency**.
2. Browse or filter the available records.
3. Select a record to view its description and open the published document when available.

### Help and legal information

Use **Help & FAQ**, **Privacy Policy**, **Terms of Use**, and **Data & Compliance** from the drawer or Profile area.

## 8. Identity Verification

Identity verification is required before using **Report**, **My Reports**, or **Online Services**. If you open one of these features while unverified, the app redirects you to the verification page.

### Submit verification

1. Open the verification page from the access prompt.
2. Enter your **Full Name**. Use at least three characters.
3. Select your **Date of Birth**.
4. Select an **ID Type**:
   - National ID
   - Driver License
   - Passport
   - Voter ID
5. Enter your **ID Number**. Use at least five characters.
6. Add the requested **ID Front Photo**, **ID Back Photo**, and **Selfie Photo** using the camera or gallery option.
7. Review the information carefully.
8. Select the submit button.

The current form can technically submit the identity record without photos, but photos are requested for review and may be necessary for approval. Use clear, readable, unedited images showing the correct document and person.

### Verification statuses

- **Pending:** The submission is waiting for administrator review. Protected features remain unavailable.
- **Verified:** The submission was approved. Reports, My Reports, and Online Services become available.
- **Rejected:** The submission was not approved. The app displays the administrator's reason when provided.

### Resubmit after rejection

1. Read the rejection reason.
2. Correct the information or replace unclear images.
3. Submit the verification again.

Resubmission changes the status back to **Pending** and clears the previous rejection reason while the new submission is reviewed.

## 9. Submit a Report

You must be verified before submitting a report.

1. Open **Report**.
2. Enter the concern or incident in **Report details**.
3. Write at least ten characters so that the issue is clear and actionable.
4. Select **Upload a photo** to add supporting images if useful. Up to five gallery images can be attached.
5. Review the message and attachments.
6. Select **Submit report**.

The report is created with the status **Pending**. Avoid submitting the same report repeatedly; the app applies a short cooldown between submissions.

### Report statuses

- **Pending:** Submitted and waiting for review.
- **Under review:** An administrator is examining the report.
- **Approved:** The report has been resolved or accepted for action.
- **Rejected:** The report was rejected. A reason may be shown.

## 10. View My Reports

1. Open **Report**.
2. Select **My Reports**.
3. Select a report to review its message, attachments, current status, and any available administrator response.

The list refreshes when you return to the page or reopen it. Report status changes may also appear in Notifications.

## 11. Request an Online Service

Online Services require identity verification. The currently active mobile catalog contains the **Document** category with these services:

- Birth Certificate
- Marriage Certificate
- Death Certificate
- CENODEATH (Certificate of No Death)

### Common steps

1. Open **Online Service**.
2. Open the **Document** category.
3. Select a service.
4. Complete the service-specific fields.
5. Provide a future appointment date and an appointment time.
6. Check the contact information and notes.
7. Submit the request.

Common fields include **Contact Number**, **Email Address**, **Purpose of Request**, **Appointment Date**, **Appointment Time**, and **Additional Notes**.

### Service-specific fields

- **Birth Certificate:** Full Name and Relationship to Certificate Owner.
- **Marriage Certificate:** Husband Name, Wife Name, and Relationship to Certificate Owner.
- **Death Certificate:** Deceased Full Name, Date of Death, Place of Death, Requester Full Name, and Relationship to Deceased.
- **CENODEATH:** Relationship to Certificate Owner plus the common request fields.

The appointment date must be in the future. Verify the email and phone number because administrators may use them when processing the request.

### Service request statuses

- **Pending:** Request received and waiting for processing.
- **Approved:** Request accepted in the mobile status view.
- **Rejected:** Request declined. A reason or note may be available.

The administrative dashboard may display the same request as **Pending**, **Processing**, **Completed**, or **Rejected**. The mobile app maps the available results into its user-facing status labels.

## 12. Notifications

1. Open **Notifications** from the notification bell or drawer.
2. Review recent updates.
3. Pull down to refresh.
4. Select **Clear all** when you want to remove the local notification list.

Notifications can relate to news, report status, online service status, or identity verification. A notification is feedback about a record; always open the related page for the latest details.

## 13. Profile and Settings

From **Profile**, you can view or edit:

- Username
- Email
- Phone Number
- Address

You can also:

- Change your password
- Open FAQs and legal pages
- Toggle **Dark Mode** in Settings
- Sign out

Keep your phone number and email current so you can be contacted about requests.

## 14. Sign Out

1. Open the drawer or Profile page.
2. Select **Logout** or **Sign out**.
3. Confirm if prompted.

Sign out after using a shared device. Never share your password or identity document images through unofficial channels.

## 15. Troubleshooting

### I cannot open Reports or Online Services

Open identity verification, complete the form, and wait for the status to become **Verified**. Pending submissions do not unlock protected features.

### My verification was rejected

Read the reason, correct the information, provide clearer document images, and resubmit.

### My report did not submit

Check the internet connection, make sure the message has at least ten characters, remove any unsupported or inaccessible image, and try again after the submission cooldown.

### My service request is not visible

Refresh the service page or reopen it. Confirm that the request was submitted while signed in and that the app has network access.

### Content is missing or old

Refresh the page. Some content can be shown from local cache when the network is unavailable; cached content may not be current.

### I cannot sign in

Check the email and password, use password reset if needed, and confirm that the account has not been blocked by an administrator.

## 16. Privacy and Safety

Only submit information needed for the service. Identity documents and report images may contain sensitive information. Use your own account, sign out on shared devices, and report suspicious requests to the municipality through an official channel.
